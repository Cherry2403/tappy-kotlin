AMSTERDAM · SINCE 1662
01
Tax by width
Long ago, tax was based on how wide the front of the house was. So people built the front skinny, and made the house long in the back instead.
02
House leans forward
Staircases are far Houses lean on purpose so furniture pulled up by rope swings away from the wall, and rain drips off the front instead of soaking it. They also lean from age: wood poles in the mud rot and sink unevenly, and old wood and brick sag after centuries.too steep for furniture. Sofas and beds still go up on a pulley and in through the window.
